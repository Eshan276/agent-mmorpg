Movement

Move up (W / Arrow Up)
Move down (S / Arrow Down)
Move left (A / Arrow Left)
Move right (D / Arrow Right)
World interaction (E / Space / Enter)

Pick up world item (facing or walking over it)
Open chest (facing, unopened)
Inspect empty chest (facing, already opened)
Talk to non-merchant NPC (shows dialog)
Open merchant shop (facing merchant NPC)
Read sign
Harvest tree (facing, with axe in inventory)
Harvest rock node (facing, with pickaxe in inventory)
Harvest bush (facing, no tool needed)
Attempt harvest without required tool (shows "Need a axe/pickaxe" message)
Attempt interact with 0 energy (blocked, energy bar flashes)
Inventory (I key)

Open inventory panel
Close inventory panel
Hover slot → see item name + description tooltip
Click usable slot (potion / food) → use item, restore HP/energy, consume one
Click non-usable slot (weapon, resource) → no effect
Shop (open by talking to merchant)

Switch to Buy tab
Switch to Sell tab
Buy item (deducts gold coins, adds item to inventory)
Attempt buy with insufficient gold (shows error feedback)
Sell inventory item (adds gold coins, removes item from inventory)
Close shop (✕ button or Escape)
Stats panel (☰ hamburger, top-right)

Open stats panel (slides in)
Close stats panel (slides out)
Passive / automatic

Walk onto item tile → auto-pickup
Enter hostile zone (Forest of Whispers / Sunken Sands Desert / Mountain Pass) → HP and energy drain every 20s
Stay in safe zone (Shinobi Village / Crystal Lake) → HP and energy regen every 20s
Passive hunger → -1 HP every 2 minutes regardless of zone
HP reaches 0 → die (sprite flashes, death overlay appears)
Respawn (Space or E while dead) → full HP/energy restore, teleport to spawn
System / meta

Game loads → spawn at Shinobi Village with empty inventory
Enter new zone → zone banner fades in at top, zone label updates bottom-right
Pick up item / open chest → inventory updated, panel reflects immediately
Harvest node depleted → sprite fades out, respawns after 15s (bush) / 30s (tree) / 60s (rock)
World items respawn continuously up to cap of 20 on the map
window.performAction('move_right') etc. — bot/devtools API works for all move + interact actions
window.getGameState() — read current player tile, direction, zone from console
